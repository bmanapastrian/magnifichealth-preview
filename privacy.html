Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Magnific Health ("we", "us") is the wellness practice of Irene Padilla, based in College Park, Maryland. This policy explains what information we collect when you use magnifichealth.world, submit one of our forms, or message us on Facebook or Instagram, and how that information is used. Questions: info@magnifichealth.world.

Information we collect

Information you give us. When you request our free Magnesium Reset Guide, join our newsletter, answer questions in a form or chat, or book a call, you may share your name, email address, phone number, and your answers about your interests and goals (for example, whether you want help with sleep, stress, or energy). Please do not send medical records or detailed health histories through forms or social media messages.

Information collected automatically. Our website uses analytics and advertising cookies (including Google Analytics and Meta tools) that record pages visited, links clicked, and general device and location information, so we can understand which content helps people and improve our services.

How we use your information

  • To deliver what you requested, such as the free guide or a booked call.
  • To respond to your messages and follow up about programs you asked about, including our community and 1:1 sessions.
  • To send our newsletter and occasional updates, if you subscribed. You can unsubscribe at any time.
  • To measure and improve our website, content, and advertising.

We do not sell your personal information.

Who we share it with

We share information only with the service providers that run the tools you interact with - for example Meta (Facebook and Instagram forms and messages), Manychat (chat automation), Beehiiv (newsletter), and Calendly (booking) - and only so they can provide those services. These providers process information under their own privacy policies.

Wellness information, not medical care

Magnific Health provides wellness education and coaching. Nothing on this website, in our forms, or in our messages is medical advice, and contacting us does not create a doctor-patient relationship. For medical concerns, please consult your physician.

Your choices

You may ask us at any time what information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or opt out of follow-up messages. Write to info@magnifichealth.world and we will act on your request promptly. You can also unsubscribe from email using the link in any newsletter, and reply STOP in chat to stop chat messages.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated date.
