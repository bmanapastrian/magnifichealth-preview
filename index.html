Frequently asked questions

What is root-cause wellness at Magnific Health?

At Magnific Health, root-cause wellness means education focused on magnesium and mineral balance, nervous system regulation, sleep, anxiety, and fatigue. Irene Padilla helps visitors explore these topics through a free guide, community content, and paid services.

Who is Magnific Health for?

Magnific Health primarily speaks to high-functioning women, roughly ages 40 to 65, who feel unwell even when their labs are described as normal. The Kids Oral Health Protocol is a separate digital offer for parents.

What do I receive when I subscribe?

You receive immediate on-site access to the free Magnesium Reset Guide, an email with the guide, and Irene's weekly notes. The same signup also puts you on the list for news of Irene's upcoming book. Check your email to confirm your subscription.

Is the community free?

The Magnific Health community has a free tier and paid Membership. The separate one-week paid Membership trial requires a card and automatically renews into a paid membership unless cancelled. Self-serve cancellation is available, and a reminder email is sent before the first charge.

Does the free fit call include coaching?

No. The free 15-minute online fit call checks whether working with Irene is a fit and routes qualified people to paid work. It does not include coaching or protocol advice.
