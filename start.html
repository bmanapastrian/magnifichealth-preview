Frequently asked questions

Where should I start?

Start with the free guide if you want to read first, or join the free community tier for engagement and free content. The one-week trial is a separate way to explore paid Membership.

Does the free Membership trial require a card?

Yes. The one-week Membership trial requires a card and automatically renews into a paid membership unless cancelled. Self-serve cancellation is available, and a reminder email is sent before the first charge.

How long is the free fit call?

The free fit call is 15 minutes and is held online. It checks whether paid work with Irene is a fit and does not include coaching or protocol advice.

How do I join the book waitlist?

Use the same email signup for the free Magnesium Reset Guide and Irene's weekly notes. That signup also puts you on the list for book updates. The title and publication date are not announced.

Is the Kids Oral Health Protocol free?

No. The Kids Oral Health Protocol is a separate paid digital product for parents. The Magnesium Reset Guide and the community's free tier are free entry points.
