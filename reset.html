Frequently asked questions

Is the Magnesium Reset Guide free?

Yes. The Magnesium Reset Guide is free. Signup also subscribes you to Irene's weekly notes and book updates. The guide is separate from paid Membership, paid sessions, and the Kids Oral Health Protocol.

How do I access the guide?

After signup, you land immediately on /guide.html to read the guide on the website. You also receive it by email. Check your email for the subscription confirmation message.

Is this a seven-day reset?

No. The guide contains a four-week reset protocol. The 7-Day Challenge is a separate community event and is not the guide's reset protocol.

Which magnesium forms does the guide cover?

The guide covers magnesium glycinate, citrate, malate, and taurate. Its supplement section includes Irene's caution concerning copper and ceruloplasmin. Read that caution with the full supplement section.

Will this guide diagnose a magnesium deficiency?

No. The guide provides wellness education, including nine signs discussed in relation to deficiency. Reading a symptom list does not diagnose a deficiency or replace advice from a qualified healthcare professional.
