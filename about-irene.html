Why did Irene begin this work?

Irene's approach grew from her own experience of feeling exhausted, anxious, and unable to sleep well while being told her labs were fine. That experience shapes the way she speaks to women who feel a gap between what they are told and how they feel.

Irene's own words:

"Before I was a practitioner, I was a woman sitting exactly where you might be right now - exhausted, anxious, sleeping poorly, and being told my labs were fine. They weren't fine. I wasn't fine. And no one was connecting the dots."

What is Irene's professional background?

Irene is a clinician and clinical psychologist by training. Her background includes an MPH from George Washington University, BS and MA degrees in Clinical Psychology from UNED in Spain, Root Cause Protocol consulting, and certifications in EFT/tapping and biomagnetism.
