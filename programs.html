Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between the free community and Membership?

The free community tier provides engagement and free content. Membership is the paid option inside the same community. Its separate one-week trial requires a card and automatically renews into a paid membership unless cancelled.

What are the monthly Membership prices?

The Membership anchor price is $97 per month. A $25-per-month founding launch price is planned for a limited founding cohort. The applicable renewal price and founding terms must be shown before trial enrollment.

What does Annual Membership include?

Annual Membership includes 12 months of Membership paid upfront and one free 90-minute session with Irene. Monthly members may buy sessions at member pricing inside the community.

How much is a 90-minute session with Irene?

The public price for a 90-minute session with Irene is $375. Monthly members can find member pricing inside the community. Annual Membership includes one free 90-minute session.

How are multi-session packages priced?

Package pricing is shared on your fit call. The free 15-minute call checks fit and routes qualified people to paid work; it does not include coaching or protocol advice.
